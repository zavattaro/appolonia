Appolonia Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23.11.2026 · English translation of the Polish “Polityka prywatności”; in case of discrepancy, the Polish version prevails.

1. Data controller

The data controller within the meaning of the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council) is: 70.144.239 ENIO ZAVATTARO FERNANDES (sole proprietorship — MEI, Brazil), email: appolonia.modlitwa@gmail.com. For all privacy matters you can contact us at the email address above.

2. Key points

  • The app runs entirely on your device, including without internet access.
  • We do not send any of your data to our servers or anyone else's.
  • We do not use tracking cookies or ads.
  • Optional analytics is disabled by default and is currently not implemented at all.
  • You can export or delete all your data at any time.

3. What data the app stores

The app stores only the following information, exclusively in the local storage of your browser/device (localStorage):

  • consent — whether you accepted the terms, the date of consent and the optional analytics setting;
  • favorites — the list of sessions marked as favorites;
  • progress — number of completed sessions, total prayer time, day streak, date of the last session;
  • settings — e.g. bell sound, text size, onboarding completion, subscription status and license key.

This data does not leave your device. We have no access to it and cannot read or recover it.

4. Data about religious beliefs

Information about which prayers you say and how often may reveal religious beliefs. Under Article 9 GDPR these are special categories of personal data, subject to enhanced protection. That is exactly why we designed the app so that no such data ever leaves the device — it is not transmitted, profiled or shared anywhere.

5. Legal basis

Storing data on the device is necessary to provide the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and, as regards data that may reveal religious beliefs, it is based on your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), given at first launch. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the data in the app settings.

6. Cookies and analytics

The app does not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels or third-party analytics tools. The consent screen includes an analytics option, which is disabled by default; in the current version of the app it is not implemented, so even if enabled no data is sent. If we introduce anonymous analytics in the future, we will update this policy and ask for separate consent.

7. Subscriptions

Purchases in the web version are handled by Paddle.com Market Limited (United Kingdom) as the seller (Merchant of Record): Paddle processes your email address, payment data and tax settlement in accordance with its own privacy policy (paddle.com/legal/privacy). The Paddle payment script loads only after you click the purchase button. After purchase you receive a license key; the key contains only a hash of part of your email address, not the address itself, and is verified solely on your device. In apps from app stores, payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store under their own policies; we do not receive your card number. While payments are not yet configured, the purchase is only simulated.

8. Your rights (Articles 15–21 GDPR)

  • Right of access (Art. 15) and right to data portability (Art. 20) — in the app settings use the “Eksportuj moje dane” (Export my data) button to download all stored information in JSON format.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16) — you can change settings and favorites directly in the app.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17) — the “Usuń wszystkie dane” (Delete all data) button in settings permanently deletes all app data from the device. The same happens when you uninstall the app or clear your browser data.
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18) and right to object (Art. 21) — since data is processed only locally, you can exercise these by deleting it or by stopping using the app.
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych) (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland, uodo.gov.pl).

9. Age of users

The app is intended for persons aged 16 or over. Younger persons may use it only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

10. Security

Because data is stored on your device, its security also depends on how your device is protected (screen lock, system updates). We recommend using these safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

We will inform you of material changes to this policy in the app. The current version is always available in the app settings.
