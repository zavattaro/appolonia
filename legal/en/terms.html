§2. Nature of the service

Appolonia is an app for guided prayer and Christian meditation in Polish. It offers sessions with text displayed step by step, a timer and sound signals (bells): the Rosary, the Chaplet of Divine Mercy, the Angelus, the examination of conscience, lectio divina, psalms, evening prayer and silent prayer. The app runs on the user's device, including without internet access.

§3. Important health disclaimer

The app is not a medical device within the meaning of the MDR and does not replace the advice of a doctor or psychotherapist.

The content is religious and spiritual in nature. If you are struggling with mental or physical health problems, contact a doctor or specialist. In a life-threatening emergency, call the emergency number 112. Mental health crisis support in Poland: 116 123 (adults) or 116 111 (children and young people).

  • Passages of Holy Scripture are the provider's own paraphrases, faithful to the meaning of the original and marked in the reference with the word “parafraza” (paraphrase); they are not quotations from any Bible translation.
  • The prayers (including the Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be, Apostles' Creed, Angelus and Chaplet of Divine Mercy) are traditional prayers of the Church.
  • All other texts (introductions, reflections, guidance) are the provider's own works.
  • The app is not an official publication of the Catholic Church and does not replace the sacraments or spiritual direction.
