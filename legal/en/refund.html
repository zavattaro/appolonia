Appolonia Refund Policy

Last updated: 23.11.2026 · Draft — subject to legal review

In short: if you ask for a refund within 14 days of your first purchase, we will refund the full amount — no reason needed. You can cancel a subscription at any time, and if you cancel the free trial before it ends, you pay nothing.

2. Statutory right of withdrawal (14 days)

A consumer in the European Union (and in the EEA, Switzerland and the United Kingdom) may withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days without giving any reason (Polish Consumer Rights Act of 30 May 2014; Directive 2011/83/EU). The period runs from the date of purchase; for a plan with a free trial — in line with Paddle's rules — from the first payment after the trial ends.

For digital content supplied immediately, this right may be lost if, at checkout, you expressly consented to the supply starting before the end of the 14-day period and acknowledged that you would thereby lose your right of withdrawal (Article 38 of the Polish Consumer Rights Act).

3. Our good-faith refund guarantee

Regardless of section 2 — even if the right of withdrawal has formally lapsed because you have started using Premium — we will refund the full amount if you ask within 14 days of your first purchase of a given plan (for the yearly plan: of the first payment after the free trial). This guarantee does not cover obvious abuse, e.g. repeatedly buying and refunding the same plan.

4. Subscriptions (monthly and yearly plans)

  • You can cancel your subscription at any time — via the link in your purchase confirmation email or at paddle.net.
  • Cancellation stops future payments and takes effect at the end of the current billing period; Premium access continues until the end of the paid period.
  • Except as described in sections 2 and 3, we do not provide pro-rata refunds for a billing period that has already started, unless required by law.

5. Yearly plan with a 7-day free trial

The yearly plan (89 zł/year) starts with a 7-day free trial. If you cancel before the trial ends, you will not be charged. If you do not cancel, Paddle will charge 89 zł after 7 days — you then have 14 days from that payment to request a full refund (section 3).

6. Lifetime plan (one-time)

The “Na zawsze” (Lifetime) plan (179 zł one-time) is a single payment with no renewal. We refund the full amount if you request a refund within 14 days of purchase.

7. How to request a refund

  • Through Paddle: use the link in your purchase confirmation email or on your Paddle receipt, or go to paddle.net, look up your order by email address and choose to request a refund.
  • By email to us: appolonia.modlitwa@gmail.com — include the email address used for the purchase and the approximate purchase date (or the order number from Paddle's email). We will forward the request to Paddle and make sure the refund is processed.

8. Timelines

  • We reply to messages within 3 business days.
  • Paddle refunds to the original payment method, usually within 14 days of approving the refund or receiving the withdrawal notice; the time it takes to appear on your statement depends on your bank or payment provider.
  • After a refund, the license key from that purchase becomes invalid and Premium access ends. Free sessions remain available.

9. Complaints and disputes

If the app does not work properly, write to us — the complaints procedure is described in §9 of the Terms of Service. Before disputing a transaction with your bank (chargeback), please contact us or Paddle — we can usually resolve it faster.
